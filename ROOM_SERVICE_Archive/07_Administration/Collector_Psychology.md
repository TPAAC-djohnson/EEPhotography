# ROOM SERVICE — Collector Psychology

## Core Principle

Collectors do not buy photographs first. They buy emotional certainty, cultural meaning, rarity, and confidence in the artist's direction.

ROOM SERVICE should be presented as a serious body of work with limited access, controlled language, and clear acquisition structure.

## What Collectors Respond To

1. **Meaning** — The work must clearly stand for something larger than decoration.
2. **Rarity** — Limited editions create urgency and seriousness.
3. **Confidence** — The artist must speak calmly and directly about value.
4. **Cohesion** — The series must feel intentional, not random.
5. **Documentation** — Certificates, edition numbers, print specs, and archive language build trust.

## Acquisition Language

Use:
- "This work is available as an archival pigment print."
- "The edition is limited to 10, with 2 artist proofs."
- "Collector sets are available by request."
- "I can provide a certificate of authenticity."
- "The recommended presentation size is 24 x 36 inches."

Avoid:
- "I sell prints."
- "Let me know if you want one."
- "I can make it cheaper."
- "I just started doing this."
- "I need support."

## Buyer Conversation Script

Collector: "How much are your prints?"

Artist: "The ROOM SERVICE works are available as limited archival pigment prints. Study prints begin in the $400 to $800 range, and gallery-scale prints are typically $800 to $1,500 depending on size and presentation. Collector sets are available by request."

Collector: "Why that price?"

Artist: "The pricing reflects the limited edition structure, archival print process, and the role of this series as a cohesive body of work intended for exhibition and long-term documentation."

Collector: "Can I get a discount?"

Artist: "I keep pricing consistent to protect the value of the work and the collectors who acquire it. I can help identify the right size or format for your budget."

Collector: "Which piece should I buy?"

Artist: "It depends on what you want the work to do in the room. If you want presence and voice, I recommend Voice. If you want reflection and legacy, I recommend What Remains. If you want tension and intimacy, I recommend Internal Weight."

## Scarcity Strategy

Use limited access language carefully:

- "Select works may be withheld from public listing for private collectors."
- "The first release will be limited."
- "Not every image from the series will be available immediately."
- "Collectors may request access to the full portfolio."

Do not fake scarcity. Scarcity must be tied to edition size, release timing, or private portfolio access.
