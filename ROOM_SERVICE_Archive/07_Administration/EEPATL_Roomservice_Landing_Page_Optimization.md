# Design Direction

## Visual Style

- Black background
- Gold accent line or border
- Large cinematic title
- Minimal text
- One hero image or dark gradient if image is not ready
- No clutter
- No social media icons above the fold

## Button Labels

Use:

- Enter the Experience
- Collector Inquiry
- Apply for Consideration

Avoid:

- Click here
- Learn more
- Submit
- Book now

---

# Conversion Logic

The page should drive visitors into one of three actions:

1. View the ROOM SERVICE experience
2. Request collector information
3. Apply for Fifty Shades of Black consideration

Do not overload the page with too many links. The page should feel selective.
