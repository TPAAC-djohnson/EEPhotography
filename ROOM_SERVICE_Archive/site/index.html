ROOM SERVICE

She Is Alone, But Never Off Stage

A photographic series documenting the private emotional environments of Black women through intimate portraiture, controlled lighting, and psychological stillness.

View Statement

Artist Statement

ROOM SERVICE is a photographic series exploring the private emotional and psychological lives of Black women within intimate, domestic spaces. Each image captures a moment of stillness that exists between authenticity and performance—where the subject is both alone and aware of being seen.

Through controlled lighting, close proximity, and recurring spatial environments, the work positions the private room as a stage. Within this space, expressions of softness, sensuality, tension, and endurance unfold without external narrative or interruption.

The series challenges dominant perceptions of Black femininity by shifting focus away from public strength and toward interior complexity. It asks the viewer to witness what is rarely documented: the quiet negotiations of identity that occur behind closed doors.

About the Artist

Darryl Johnson is a multidisciplinary visual artist, researcher, and director based in Atlanta, Georgia. His work explores identity, intimacy, psychological presence, and cultural documentation within underrepresented communities.

As founder of The Path: An Agent of Change, Inc., Johnson brings a research-informed and socially engaged lens to his creative work, positioning photography as both art and archive.
